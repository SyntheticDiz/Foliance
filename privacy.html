PRIVACY, IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Your documents stay
on your Mac.

Foliance is designed for local document work. This page explains what stays in the app, what you choose to share, and what is separate about this support website.

Effective 30 October 2026

ON THIS PAGE App data AI processing Saved and exported files This website Your choices
01

Data in the app

Foliance reads PDFs that you choose. Document text, summaries, questions, notes, selected passages, recent-file bookmarks, and saved work remain in Foliance's own local app container on your Mac.

Foliance has no account system, advertisements, tracking, analytics SDK, cloud synchronization, or embedded credentials. The app does not send your documents to a developer server or a third-party AI service.

02

On-device AI processing

AI analysis uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models. It requires a compatible Apple silicon Mac, macOS 26 or later, Apple Intelligence enabled, and a downloaded model that supports the selected language.

Apple may require an internet connection to download its system model. Apple handles system downloads and diagnostics under its own policies. Reading, local search, and reviewing available results do not require sending document content to a cloud AI service.

AI interpretations are not verified facts. Check findings and quotations against the original PDF.

03

Saved work and exports

You choose where to save exported PDFs and portable .foliance work files. Work files contain the original PDF together with analysis and annotations. Anyone who receives one may be able to read that content, so share it only with intended recipients.

Local application data and caches are held in Foliance's sandbox container. Deleting a recent-file entry removes its shortcut; it does not delete the original PDF.

Apple may separately handle App Store purchases, downloads, and system diagnostics under its own policies.

04

This support website

This website is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor request information and service logs under its own privacy terms. That hosting activity is separate from Foliance's local app data.

The site has no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. If you follow a link to GitHub to report an issue, information you submit there is handled by GitHub and the project issue tracker. Do not include a PDF, document text, credentials, or other private material in a public issue.

05

Your choices

You decide which PDFs to open, where exported files are saved, and whether to share a work file or open an external link. When you deliberately open a link in your browser, the linked website receives that request under its own privacy terms; your PDF is not attached by Foliance.

For help, visit Foliance Support. After the first release, privacy and support contact details will also be available on the App Store product page.

In brief: Foliance keeps document work on your Mac. You choose what to export or share. The hosting provider's website request handling is separate from the app.
